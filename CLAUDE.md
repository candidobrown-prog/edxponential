# BetterEd brand: LOCKED (version 2.2, approved by Candido Brown, 9 Nov 2026)

Any work for BetterEd Solutions must follow the locked brand exactly. The full book is
`bettered-site/brand/BRAND.md`; logos and icons are in `bettered-site/brand/assets/`; tokens in
`bettered-site/brand/tokens.json`. The same toolkit is published at
https://claude.ai/artifact/JLZYPoYKDEeM3dVm63iNsz. Never change a brand element without the
founder's written approval (then bump the version; see `bettered-site/README.md`, "Brand lock").

Essentials:
- Mark: the B-Cross (medical cross whose right arm forms a B; navy blocks, gold core). Use the
  files; never redraw, recolor or restyle it.
- Colors: Navy #0B1F3A, Harbor #1A5FB4, Signal gold #FFC20E, Paper #F6F4EE, Slate #4F5B6D,
  Mist #E3E9F1, Pulse #E8603C (small roles only). Square corners; no gradients or shadows.
- Type: Source Serif 4 (headlines, 400; italic for the accent word) and Work Sans (interface).
  Email/Office fallbacks: Georgia and Arial.
- Headline: "Schools can get well. Fast." Tagline: "Happy. Healthy. High-performing."
- EdSolutions, in order: School Health (culture & climate, the heart), Leader Development (the
  head), Teacher Development (the hands), Intensive Care (whole-school turnaround, the whole body).
- Levels of intervention: Core, Crew, Cohort, Community.
- Method: Intake, Diagnosis, Treatment plan, Treatment, Strengthen, Discharge & follow-up.
- Voice: plain, direct, urgent; "teachers" not "guides"; results are measured, not promised.
