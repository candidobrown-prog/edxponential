# BetterEd Brand Toolkit

**Status: LOCKED.** Version 2.2, adopted 9 November 2026 by Candido Brown, Founder and CEO. This is the official BetterEd brand. Use it exactly as written. Nothing here changes without the founder's written approval, recorded as a new version at the end of this book.

BetterEd Solutions is a school improvement, turnaround and transformation firm that works like a modern medical institution: we diagnose honestly, treat urgently, and stay until the results hold.

## The mark: the B-Cross

A medical cross whose right arm becomes the two bowls of a B. Navy blocks surround a gold core.

- **The cross** says care, health and medicine.
- **The B** says BetterEd. Read one way it is a cross; read the other it is a B.
- **The gold core** is the diagnosis, at the heart of everything.

**Lockups** (see Logos): the primary lockup is the B-Cross with "BetterEd" in Source Serif 4 Semibold and "SOLUTIONS" in tracked Work Sans. Use the reversed lockup on Navy. The mark alone is for icons, avatars and small spaces.

- **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least one block (one third of the mark's width) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** lockup 140 px wide on screen (1.25 in in print); mark 16 px.
- **Never:** stretch, outline, add effects, change the core from gold, put the navy mark on a dark ground, or set the wordmark in another typeface.

## Typography

- **Source Serif 4** (400, 400 italic, 600): every headline, the lead paragraph, pull quotes, numerals, step numbers. Headlines are Regular 400, set tight (letter-spacing about −2%).
- **Work Sans** (400, 500, 600, 700): body copy, navigation, buttons and labels. Buttons and navigation are 600, uppercase, tracked about +0.1em.
- Both are open-source (SIL OFL 1.1) and the files are included here. Fallbacks: Georgia for the serif, Arial for the sans.
- In email and Office documents, where web fonts may not load, use Georgia and Arial.

## The BetterEd Model

The plain cross used as a diagram, never as the logo. Each arm is one EdSolution; the gold center is the diagnosis, which decides which arms a school needs, in what order and at what level of intervention.

## EdSolutions (always in this order)

| # | Name | Description | Body | Icon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | School Health | Culture & climate | The heart (a culture problem is a heart condition) | heart |
| 02 | Leader Development | Principals & leadership teams | The head | head |
| 03 | Teacher Development | Instruction in the classroom | The hands | hand |
| 04 | Intensive Care | Whole-school turnaround | The whole body | body |

The description always appears with the name.

## Levels of intervention

| Level | Who it reaches | Offered in |
|---|---|---|
| Core | One leader, one-to-one (the 101) | School Health, Leader Development, Intensive Care |
| Crew | A leader's team, with or without the leader | All four |
| Cohort | Leaders or teachers across campuses | All four |
| Community | The whole school | School Health, Teacher Development, Intensive Care |

Each level has a block glyph that fills one square per level of reach (see Icons).

## The method: six steps

Intake → Diagnosis → Treatment plan → Treatment → Strengthen → Discharge & follow-up.

## Iconography

Four EdSolution icons (heart, head, hand, body) drawn on a 32 px grid with a 1.8 px Navy line, square caps, and a gold plus for care. Four level glyphs. Use only these; do not mix in other icon sets.

## Voice

- Plain, direct and urgent. Short sentences. Medical language used precisely: diagnose, treat, vital signs, discharge.
- Children are never the patient; the institution is.
- Say "teachers", never "guides". Write "MASTERY", never "M.A.S.T.E.R.Y.".
- Results are "measured, not promised": every number carries its source and limits.
- Do not promise timelines unless the founder supplies them.

## Photography and video

Real schools, leaders and classrooms in natural light. Licensed images with model releases for anyone identifiable; never present stock images as BetterEd clients. Background video is muted, slow, 10 to 20 seconds, and always pausable.

## Change control

This brand is locked. The website enforces it: its build stops if the logo files, icons, fonts or color and type tokens change without an approved update. To change anything, the founder approves it in writing, the version number goes up, and the change is recorded here.
