Install it

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail on a computer, click the gear, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "BetterEd".
  4. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose "BetterEd" for new emails and for replies.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Delete your old signature first, and don't paste on top of it: leftover formatting is what usually jumbles a signature.

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook (web or new Outlook), open Settings, then Account, then Signatures.
  3. Click New signature, name it "BetterEd", and paste into the box.
  4. Choose it as the default for new messages and for replies and forwards, then click Save.

Classic Outlook for Windows: File, Options, Mail, Signatures. If pasting loses the layout there, use Copy HTML code and ask whoever manages your Microsoft 365 to install it as an HTML signature.

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Mail on your Mac, open Mail, then Settings, then Signatures.
  3. Pick your BetterEd account, click +, name it "BetterEd", and paste into the right-hand box.
  4. Untick Always match my default message font, so the signature keeps its own type.

The logo is hosted online. If Mail shows it as a blank box at first, it appears once the message is sent.
