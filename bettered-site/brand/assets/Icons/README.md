# Icons

**EdSolution icons**, 32 px grid, 1.8 px Navy (#0B1F3A) line, square caps, gold (#FFC20E) plus for care:

- `heart.svg`: School Health (culture & climate), the heart
- `head.svg`: Leader Development, the head
- `hand.svg`: Teacher Development, the hands
- `body.svg`: Intensive Care (whole-school turnaround), the whole body

On Navy, draw the line in white and keep the plus gold.

**Level glyphs**, a 2 × 2 grid that fills one block per level of reach: `level-core.svg` (one), `level-crew.svg` (two), `level-cohort.svg` (three), `level-community.svg` (four).

Use only these icons. Do not mix in other icon sets.
