## Historical (version 1, 7 to 8 November 2026)

The numbered files `01-…` to `08-…` record the original build: sources, red-team reviews, strategy, design system, QA and launch dossier. They describe the first design, which used the Vital Line mark, Libre Franklin and Newsreader, and the earlier EdSolution names (Culture & Climate, Leadership Development, Educator Development, Whole-School Turnaround). They are kept as a record of the reasoning. Where they disagree with `../brand/BRAND.md` or the live templates, the brand book and the templates win.

What changed since version 1:

| Version 1 | Now |
|---|---|
| Vital Line (heartbeat) mark | B-Cross mark; logo is the B-Cross + "BetterEd" in Arial Bold + a gold square period |
| Libre Franklin and Newsreader | Work Sans and Source Serif 4 |
| "Schools can get well." | "Schools can get well. Fast." |
| Culture & Climate, Leadership Development, Educator Development, Whole-School Turnaround | School Health, Leader Development, Teacher Development, Intensive Care |
| Four stages: Diagnose, Treat, Strengthen, Sustain | Six steps: Intake, Diagnosis, Treatment plan, Treatment, Strengthen, Discharge & follow-up |
| School Health Index open via the contact form | Free account required; one index per person; report in 24 to 48 hours |
| No levels | Levels of intervention: Core, Crew, Cohort, Community |
