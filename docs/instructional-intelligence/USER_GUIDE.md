# User Guide — Instructional Intelligence

## Coaches and observers
1. **New Observation** → six short screens (context, learning context, classroom context, evidence, authorization, begin). Your draft is saved on your device.
2. **Live Audio Recording**: big Record button, pause/resume, stop & save. Mark evidence with one tap (teacher question, student response, misconception, instructional adjustment, engagement, uncertain) — timestamps are captured for you. Audio is uploaded every 10 seconds; "Recording saved" appears only after the server has verified every piece. Keep the tab in the foreground on phones.
3. **Transcript Review**: click a line to jump the player; correct words and speaker labels; mark inaudible/overlap; rename speaker slots (Student 1, Student 2 …). Save as a new version — originals are kept. Approve when ready.
4. **Instructional Analysis**: run analysis. Tabs: Overview, Questions (Bloom's, DOK, demand, alignment), Student Responses (correctness vs reasoning), Discourse (talk time, participation), Engagement (domains + coverage), CFU-100 (evidence-linked recommendations you can correct), Misconceptions (hypotheses with competing explanations), Next Steps (≤ 3 priorities). Every finding links to its timestamp; quotes that are not verbatim in the transcript are flagged.
5. **Student Work & Exit Tickets**: enter deidentified results with a denominator; mastery risk updates.
6. **Sharing & Access**: share with the teacher, with school leadership, or grant a named person. Nothing is visible to the teacher until you share.
7. **Coaching Conference**: evidence left, decisions right; up to three priorities; commitments with owner and date; both people acknowledge (disagreement is recorded). Create the action plan from the conference; schedule reassessments.
8. **Reports**: Executive summary or Full evidence report; print to PDF. Reports always state limitations and review status.

## Teachers
- **Teacher Reflection**: eight areas, one at a time, with optional voice dictation and an AI follow-up that asks for specific evidence. Reflections are private; you decide whom to share with and can revoke. A reflection can be done without any recording.
- Observations shared with you show the recording, transcript and findings; you can read, respond in the coaching conference, add evidence of your practice change, and record reassessment results.

## Everyone
- Student identities are never recorded: speaker slots are anonymous. Do not type names.
- Scores here are developmental coaching information, not personnel evaluation.
- If something cannot be scored, the system says so rather than inventing a number.
